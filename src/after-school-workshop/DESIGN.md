# After School Workshop

A young adult sketches in a sunlit courtyard after class, with a poppy city bicycle, quiet cobalt accents and clean ivory surfaces.

Home uses a tightly bounded story illustration in the large Editorial cover pane, with contain geometry preserving the face and hands. Light and dark wallpaper have no repeated subject and add only quiet lower-edge geometry. Startup and empty states use a separate transparent character pose rather than an object. All eleven canonical decoration slots and seven native glyphs are explicit, including complete compact/regular objects. Native acceptance screenshots are recorded separately; the gallery preview is a fictional UI composition.

Native tablet review found that the Home title After Class overlapped the figure’s head and shoulder. The Home title is now Sketch; the full theme name remains After School Workshop and the artwork scale is preserved.
