## Retained-asset notes

The three speed-fin/paperclip glyphs are original generated control designs; the four `native-*` glyphs are original geometric drawings by osuki-dev. The composer corner is the original generated coastal leaf, wave and ring ornament from 2026-10-13. The parameters glyph has the separate Sea Glass Morning attribution above. None of these retained files depicts the former Sonic character. Their prior notices are preserved by the historical source link; this refresh does not relicense them or assert franchise permission.

The prior character hero, empty illustration, four scenic character wallpapers and preview are replaced. Sonic the Hedgehog rights remain with SEGA; no license or endorsement is asserted. That historical franchise notice does not describe the new adult skating group. Version 1.1.9 corrects the obsolete manifest license label without changing artwork. The historical 1.1.8 frozen package is preserved unchanged.
