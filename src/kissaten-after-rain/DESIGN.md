# Kissaten After Rain

## Direction

a quiet kissaten after rainfall. The interface uses coffee amber, bottle green, aged cream. Light and dark variants are separately illustrated and art-directed rather than mechanically recolored.

## Home

The home identity uses a short Japanese slogan. The hero is a transparent siphon coffee service, composed with generous transparent padding for both the home view and the app launch screen. No home decoration layer is used.

## Chrome

Back, send, and attach are custom monochrome template icons matched to the theme's line weight.
