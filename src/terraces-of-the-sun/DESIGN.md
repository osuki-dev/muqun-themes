# Terraces of the Sun

An original Peruvian Andes landscape interpreted as tightly woven fields. Curved agricultural terraces provide the visual rhythm, with angular ridges and a mineral river creating depth. Quinoa cream surfaces and oxblood actions distinguish daylight; deep claret surfaces and turquoise actions distinguish night.

Four separately composed wallpapers support compact and regular layouts. The center and upper sky remain quiet behind content. Default Muqun identity is preserved. Surface opacity is 96 percent to protect legibility over the textile detail.

Template glyphs use terrace steps for back, a soaring directional condor for send, and a stepped paperclip for attachments. Alpha supplies their shape; the active palette supplies color.

The 1024x640 gallery cover presents light artwork on the left and dark artwork on the right. Artwork and transparent glyphs were inspected, including template tinting at 17px and 34px. CLI 1.8.0 validates all nine assets and package round-trip; the source catalogue check passes. The shared interface opacity floor is 90% and terminal floor 93%, below the authored 96%.
