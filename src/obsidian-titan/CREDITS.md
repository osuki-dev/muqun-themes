# Obsidian Titan artwork

All artwork was originally generated for Muqun using the built-in OpenAI image generation tool on 2026-09-26. No third-party characters, logos or downloaded artwork were used. Assets are distributed under CC0-1.0.

- `artwork.webp`: original silver-haired volcanic pilot with obsidian guardian, transparent Home cutout, shared between light and dark.
- `launch.webp`: separately generated complete pilot and guardian launch composition.
- `wallpaper-light.webp`, `wallpaper-dark.webp`: original character-free volcanic landscapes.
- `preview.png`: gallery composition of this theme's own imagery.
- `icon-*.png`: seven original vector-authored semantic glyphs rasterized to transparent PNG, theme-tinted. The arrow is drawn up-right.

## Art direction

Premium original anime illustration; silver hair, amber eyes, basalt segmented armor, ember scarf and gigantic volcanic guardian. Home keeps left-middle transparent for native controls. Launch preserves the complete pilot. Light wallpaper has pale ash clouds and a vivid lower caldera; dark uses warm charcoal and glowing lava, never navy. No lettering is baked into Home, launch or wallpaper.

## Effect

Restrained rising embers at intensity 0.18, speed 0.3, density 0.35 and size 0.7. Palette uses primary and warning roles. Uses the App's existing bounded effect renderer; no shaders or scripts are bundled.

## Version 1.0.1 framing correction

Home and launch were outpainted with the built-in image tool to complete the physical silhouette instead of hiding canvas cuts with layout changes. Home retains the close-up pilot and titan as a complete pointed armored bust, with all scarf tips enclosed. Launch preserves the boots and finishes the entire volcanic ledge. Opaque light and dark review composites verify the canvas boundaries.
