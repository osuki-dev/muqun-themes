# Tetromino Atelier

A Tetris-inspired geometric study using tactile matte block sculptures, shallow relief and orthographic composition. Cobalt, tangerine and lemon pieces sit against warm white or deep graphite. Broad negative space keeps the workspace calm.

Dedicated portrait and landscape wallpaper preserve the geometric composition. Shared wallpaper is restrained behind reading surfaces while Home retains stronger artwork. A contained block sculpture is declared for Home hero and empty states. Default Muqun identity is retained.

Stepped arrows and a rectilinear paperclip give the controls a related geometry. Complete UI and ANSI palettes distinguish actions and semantic roles in both modes. The preview pairs light on the left with dark on the right.

The dedicated Home hero presents an architectural tetromino arch and stair pavilion. It is a separate transparent composition from the preserved empty-state illustration and is used in both modes and responsive sizes.
