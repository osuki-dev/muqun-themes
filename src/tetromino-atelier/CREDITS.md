## Parameters glyph refresh

`assets/icon-parameters.png` reuses the original Akiba Overdrive control glyph by Muqun: two block-ended adjustment tracks with rectangular handles. Source: [Muqun theme source](https://github.com/osuki-dev/muqun-themes/blob/main/src/akiba-overdrive/assets/icon-parameters.png). Asset license: CC0-1.0; this attribution and license apply specifically to the reused glyph. The raster is copied unchanged with its transparent alpha intact. `chrome.parameters` uses template rendering in both appearances and remains independent of `chrome.settings`.
