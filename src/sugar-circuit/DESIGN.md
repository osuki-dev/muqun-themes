# Sugar Circuit

A peach-haired adult arcade heroine explores an original candy-plastic world. Bold cel shading, chunky capsules and clear cyan rings establish the visual language. Light mode uses milk-white and raspberry; dark mode uses deep berry shadow, raspberry-pink controls and ice-cyan information accents.

Four original wallpapers serve compact and regular layouts in both modes. The contained transparent Home banner keeps the heroine clearly present above the cards, while a separate chibi illustration serves empty states. Default Muqun identity remains visible. Decorative art is static and does not supply functional controls.

The three template glyphs are a wrapped-candy back arrow, a capsule rocket for send, and a candy-tube paperclip for attachment. The gallery cover is 1024 x 640 with light on the left and dark on the right.

## Validation

CLI 1.8.1 validation, contrast, package round trip and full source checks pass without warnings for this theme. Shared opacity floors are 92% for the interface and 90% for the terminal; both use 96% authored surface opacity. Wallpaper decoration uses 32% strength to keep conversation text clear while the contained Home banner retains full character detail.

Light and dark Phone Home, Conversation and Terminal, and Tablet Home and Terminal were visually reviewed in the official browser preview. Character placement stays within the banner and background; the composer and labels remain readable. Transparent art and all three template glyphs were inspected, including tinted 17px and 34px glyphs. Native-device import was not tested.
