## Native adaptation 1.1.4

`assets/icon-create.png`, `assets/icon-scan.png`, `assets/icon-settings.png`, and `assets/icon-arrow.png` are original vector glyphs authored for this theme and rasterized to transparent 128×128 PNG with ImageMagick. Chunky garden tools and pixel-like angular joints. No third-party source or reference artwork was used. Covered by the theme’s existing license. Existing illustrations are unchanged. `home.arrow` visibly points up-right; its direction metadata declares that source orientation.

`assets/preview.png` is a deterministic light/dark gallery composition of the existing foreground artwork, final palette and native control glyphs. It is a concept preview, not a device screenshot.
