# Amber Relay

Analog signal hardware becomes a precise screenprint in warm bone, bakelite brown and amber. Rounded oscilloscope housings, copper induction coils, cable loops and glass valves give the theme its physical character. Instrument faces carry abstract waves without labels.

Dedicated light/dark portrait and wide wallpapers hold detail near the edges. The contained Home banners present the signal hardware clearly above the server content. A transparent valve vignette serves empty states. Default Muqun home identity remains intact.

Template glyphs connect back to the artwork: a patch-cable back arrow, transmission-arc send arrow and connector paperclip. UI planes use 94% opacity and terminal planes 97% to retain readable content over the print. ANSI colors retain distinct semantic families while harmonizing with the warm palette. The gallery cover presents daylight on the left and night on the right.

Editorial Home uses the cover header with normal theme-colored toolbar surfaces and a new 2:1 alpha foreground of an oscilloscope, copper coil, valves and braided leads. It replaces the earlier opaque light/dark banner treatment; those source images remain archived beside the current assets but are no longer packaged. The same contained foreground is explicit in light and dark, compact and regular widths. Classic keeps the standalone radio hero and startup behavior unchanged.

## Toolbar surfaces

Toolbar controls use the normal theme surface. This theme does not force a transparent or specially translucent toolbar; readers can adjust surface opacity in the app.
