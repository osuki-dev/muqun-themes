Original AI-generated artwork for the Muqun Theme Collection. All rights reserved.

The woodcut landscapes interpret Lofoten fishing cabins, fjords and timber craft; they are not exact landmark reproductions. No third-party artwork is bundled. Prompts are documented in PROMPTS.md.

Context: [Visit Norway: Lofoten](https://www.visitnorway.com/places-to-go/northern-norway/the-lofoten-islands/) and [Visit Lofoten: fishermen's cabins](https://visitlofoten.com/en/topic/accommodation-in-lofoten/fishermens-cabins-and-sea-houses/).
