# Last Inning artwork

All six illustrations were generated for Muqun with OpenAI image generation on 2026-09-26. They depict an original adult baseball pitcher and independently illustrated daylight and floodlit stadiums; no existing characters, team marks or lettering were requested. Source: original generated work, no external source URL. Distributed under CC0-1.0.

The seven monochrome icons are original code-authored diamond and seam geometry by Muqun, rasterized to PNG; CC0-1.0. The gallery preview is composed from this theme’s own assets.

Home portraits and full-length launch art are independent compositions with genuine alpha. Artwork is capped at 1024 pixels on its longest edge to limit decoded memory.
