# Poolside Prism

Two tennis friends share a poolside laugh after a match. Fresh summer photography in cyan, lime and navy, with a warm evening counterpart.

One complete transparent foreground and responsive day/night environments serve Classic, Editorial and Stage. Eight authored semantic glyphs retain native fallbacks. Coordinated chrome and composer accents are retained where present in the original pack; obsolete human ornaments are replaced with the new subject. Startup reuses the complete foreground.

Source validation does not establish native phone or Pad acceptance.
