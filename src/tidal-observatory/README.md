# Tidal Observatory

Two researchers share a telescope above the ocean. Western adventure illustration in evergreen, midnight and peach, shifting from coastal daylight to moonlight.

One transparent foreground and responsive day/night environments serve Classic, Editorial and Stage. Human figures are complete from head to footwear. Native glyphs remain enabled for all actions. Composer ornaments are disabled in both modes and widths; no outset is applied. Startup reuses the complete foreground.

Source validation does not establish native phone or Pad acceptance.
