# Lucid Encore

An original star DJ conducts holographic sound ribbons in a delicate anime rhythm dream. Periwinkle, ink plum and rose gold give the world a luminous night-stage identity. The character has dark-lilac hair, star headphones and translucent ribbon accessories.

Dedicated portrait compositions keep the face low enough to remain visible behind Home content, while a contained Home banner provides a clear character portrait. Four mode/layout-specific wallpapers, a transparent chibi vignette and three rhythm-inspired template glyphs support the design. Default Muqun identity remains intact.

## Readability and validation

Home retains full-strength artwork and the contained character banner. Conversation wallpaper is reduced to 18% so bright sound ribbons do not compete with unboxed text; terminal retains a 96% plane.

CLI 1.8.1 validation, contrast, package round trip, packaged validation and full source checks passed. All ten assets are present, and this theme has no warnings. Shared opacity floors are 89% interface and 91% terminal, below the authored 96%.

Artwork and all three template glyphs were reviewed at 17px and 34px in both tints. Approved browser screenshots were inspected in both modes for Phone Home, Conversation and Terminal, plus Tablet Home and Terminal. The initial conversation wallpaper was too bright; the reduced-opacity version was rechecked and text remains readable. No native device import is claimed.
