# Lucid Encore

An original star DJ conducts holographic sound ribbons in a delicate anime rhythm dream. Periwinkle, ink plum and rose gold give the world a luminous night-stage identity. The character has dark-lilac hair, star headphones and translucent ribbon accessories.

Dedicated portrait compositions keep the face low enough to remain visible behind Home content. Editorial Home uses the cover composition: the existing transparent star DJ becomes the foreground over the mode- and width-specific dream-stage wallpaper, while the native Muqun identity and controls remain separate and readable. Classic Home and the launch overlay continue to use the same contained, wordless hero. A transparent chibi vignette and three rhythm-inspired template glyphs support the design. Default Muqun identity remains intact.

## Readability and validation

Home retains full-strength artwork, with the cover foreground kept transparent and free of baked-in labels or controls. Conversation wallpaper is reduced to 18% so bright sound ribbons do not compete with unboxed text; terminal retains a 96% plane.

CLI 1.9.0 validation, contrast, package round trip, packaged validation and full source checks passed. All eleven assets are present, and this theme has no warnings. Shared opacity floors are 89% interface and 91% terminal, below the authored 96%.

Artwork and all three template glyphs were reviewed at 17px and 34px in both tints. The transparent hero preserves its full silhouette at compact and regular widths; the wallpaper variants continue to switch between portrait and landscape compositions. No native device import is claimed.
