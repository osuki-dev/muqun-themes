# Lunar Aquarium

Recommended: Editorial Cover on phone and Pad. The narrow original portrait is contain-fit against the right edge (compact x=0.88, regular x=1), leaving the left utility and title region open. Top alignment preserves the face before the cover's native lower 32% feather. Compact cover height is 0.9×width, capped at 640; the Pad cover places artwork beside the reading column and above its launch dock. The default Muqun title stays intact and its logo is hidden. Classic and Editorial Standard remain supported: their shorter bands show the complete contained silhouette at reduced scale. Layout is a user preference, not selected by the theme.

The artwork is wordless and nonessential. Surface/terminal opacity is 1 in both modes. The local 1.0.3 candidate has been imported through the Android App file picker for phone and tablet review; keyboard-open, voice and iOS coverage are not claimed.

## Home composer waterline

The Home field carries a shallow horizontal moonlit-water ripple and small moonstones, with separate compact and regular artwork. Upright side plants are removed so wide fields no longer contain an inset frame and multiline drafts stay clear. Light/dark image opacity is 0.74/0.90. Every responsive image repeats contain fit, bottom-center alignment and 12/12/8 dp left/right/bottom outsets. A separate pair of horizontally drifting jellyfish uses the bounded 64 dp overhang, with its bottom quarter clear and left-middle alignment. Its light/dark opacity is 0.80/0.95. The eighth template icon is an orbital two-row adjustment glyph for `chrome.parameters`; it is distinct from Settings.

The drafting area and toolbar regions stay transparent. Artwork does not change the field base fill, theme opacity or saved reader choices. Gallery artwork remains free of App controls. Native phone/Pad, keyboard and voice-state acceptance must be recorded separately from source/pack validation.
