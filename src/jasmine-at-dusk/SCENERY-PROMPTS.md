# Home scenery and cover generation 1.0.11

Original fictional images made with the built-in OpenAI image generation tool for Muqun Theme Collection on 2026-10-13. License: All rights reserved. Existing collection Home paintings supplied cover-edit references; wallpaper generation used no third-party image. Final generated bytes were copied directly into assets, with no script resizing, compositing, alpha manipulation or image edits.

## Wallpaper brief

Orchid dusk sky and high jasmine branches extend above the traveler and courtyard. The page continues into faint courtyard architecture, banana leaves and jasmine around a quiet paper center.

Generate opaque handmade paper across the entire portrait canvas, with actual very-low-contrast watercolor scenery at the outermost 12–15% edges. Keep central 70% width and middle/lower reading region nearly blank. Feather forms inward without rectangular bands, borders, UI or a repeated line template. The top quarter continues the scene quietly behind native header icons. No people, food, vehicles, trains, duplicate hero subjects, text, logos or watermark. Light uses the theme background ivory/mint; dark uses theme navy/forest paper with no bright flower or lamp marks.

Generate independent light/dark regular portraits, then edit each into a 1:2 phone composition: retain the scene and paper colors, reposition shapes against the narrower outer edges rather than merely center-cropping, soften contrast by one third and retain the central reading area. Final compact images are 887×1774; regular images are 1024×1536.

## Cover edit brief

Use the existing Home scene as an edit reference. Preserve recognizable central subject identity, face or vehicle, clothing, hands and scene objects. Recompose into a complete near-square 1200×1080 scene. Extend connected sky, architecture or vegetation through the upper third to both upper edges. Keep faces and critical objects below the upper-right utility area; use quiet atmosphere behind icons. Preserve the original cel-shaded hand-ink style. Entire canvas must be painted and opaque at top, without a cutout gap, paper card, blank margin or added interface. Paint soft bottom haze for native Home feathering. Accepted source dimensions are 1322×1190.
