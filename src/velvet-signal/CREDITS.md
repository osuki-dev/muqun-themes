# Velvet Signal artwork

All illustrations are original artwork generated for Muqun with OpenAI's built-in image generation tool on 2026-09-26. No existing franchise, artist reference, stock image or external character is included. Distribution license: CC0-1.0. These original generated assets have no external source URL. Seven icons are original geometric drawings by Muqun.

## Art direction and generation record

The character is an adult noir detective with a dark chin-length bob, expressive hazel eyes, deep plum belted trench coat, ivory shirt and geometric copper diamond brooch. The final style is Western animated graphic-novel ink: clearly visible dark contours, sculpted angular facial planes and large deliberate cel-shadow shapes. Earlier realistically painted drafts were rejected and are not packaged.

- `assets/home-light.webp`: Original three-quarter close portrait, hands naturally tucked into the trench-coat pockets. The final built-in style transformation replaced photographic skin and dense texture with smooth graphic skin, bold ink contours and limited flat material tones. Complete hair, shoulders and sleeves remain within the frame. Only the lower coat fades to transparency.
- `assets/home-dark.webp`: Lighting-only edit of the final comic portrait. Warm amber contour highlights, deep plum shadows and cream shirt preserve the same identity, pose, brooch, garment layers and outline.
- `assets/launch-light.webp`: Independent complete standing illustration, matching the final Home identity and clothing. One hand holds a closed ivory pocket notebook at the waist; the other holds a small cylindrical brass flashlight. The complete coat hem, trousers and ankle boots are present. The portrait was the identity/style reference, while an independent standing draft supplied the pose; the result is not a crop of Home.
- `assets/launch-dark.webp`: Lighting-only edit of the complete launch illustration, preserving the notebook and flashlight grips, coat seams, belt, copper brooch, face and boots. Amber rim light and plum shadow blocks replace daylight.
- `assets/wallpaper-light.webp`: Original monumental Art Deco rain city in rose-ivory afternoon light: stepped limestone towers, copper crowns, elevated bridges, wet paving and warm clouds. Quiet pale upper sky supports native controls and heading. No people, signage or lettering.
- `assets/wallpaper-dark.webp`: Independently generated nighttime Deco metropolis, aubergine-black sky, amber lamps and window rhythms on wet streets. This is not a blue color filter applied to the daylight image.
- `assets/preview.png`: Deterministic 1024 x 640 side-by-side composition of this theme's own foregrounds and corresponding wallpapers.

All six illustrations were generated with the built-in image tool. Final illustrations are static WebP, at most 1024 pixels on the long edge. Generated alpha was preserved during size/format conversion. No script, SVG or raw generation draft is packaged.

## Seven original icons

The Art Deco system uses consistent geometric strokes, stepped corners and copper-brooch-inspired diamond geometry. All glyphs are authored as original paths and rasterized to 128 x 128 PNG template masks:

- `icon-back.png`: Single left return arrow with a Deco shaft detail; no opposing arrowhead.
- `icon-send.png`: Upward message dart with central fold.
- `icon-attach.png`: Open paperclip with clearly separated inner loop.
- `icon-create.png`: Central plus and four short Deco rays.
- `icon-scan.png`: Four scanning corners with a diamond focus mark.
- `icon-settings.png`: Stepped radial gear with circular hub.
- `icon-arrow.png`: Northeast navigation arrow with a squared lower corner; declared `up-right`.

The seven supported roles are `chrome.back`, `chrome.send`, `chrome.attach`, `chrome.create`, `chrome.scan`, `chrome.settings`, and `home.arrow`. Theme tinting supplies readable light/dark colors. Other native App glyphs remain native; unsupported replacement roles are not invented.

## Detail and validation review

The final Home and independent launch variants were inspected composited against pale rose and dark plum. No opaque scenic rectangle or external glow plane remains. Launch has a complete silhouette and Home has an intentional soft coat fade. Enlarged launch-hand crops were checked: notebook grip shows one thumb and four fingers, flashlight grip retains a visible thumb with fingers wrapping behind the cylinder. Coherent notebook edges, flashlight perspective, coat layers, belt buckles, brooch, eyes and matching facial identity were inspected. Occluded fingers are not artificially drawn through props.

Compact and regular slots explicitly contain the foreground. Native UI surfaces remain opaque by default. The existing bounded rain effect is deliberately subdued: intensity 0.1, density 0.15, speed 0.3. CLI 2.3.1 validation and contrast passed. Native App installation is not claimed by this artwork source review.
