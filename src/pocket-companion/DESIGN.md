# Pocket Companion

An original cocoa-furred otter with a cream muzzle and apricot neckerchief rests among smooth river pebbles. Soft pencil detail and tactile paper create a warm, quiet companion. Oat surfaces and cocoa text define the light variant; cocoa surfaces and apricot controls define the dark variant.

Four distinct wallpapers place the character near the lower edge, leaving broad quiet space. A transparent pocket greeting uses the supported home.decoration slot with contain fitting: the otter peeks over a small illustrated seam inside the banner bounds. It is a static illustration. A separate transparent sitting otter fills the empty-state slot. Default Muqun identity is preserved.

The template glyphs interpret a tail as a back arrow, a folded cloth pocket as a paper-plane send action, and a rounded clip as the attachment control. The cover is 1024 x 640 with light on the left and dark on the right.
