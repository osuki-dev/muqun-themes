## Native adaptation 1.1.4

`assets/icon-create.png`, `assets/icon-scan.png`, `assets/icon-settings.png`, and `assets/icon-arrow.png` are original vector glyphs authored for this theme and rasterized to transparent 128×128 PNG with ImageMagick. Tapered martial-arrow geometry with bold straight strokes. No third-party source or reference artwork was used. Covered by the theme’s existing license. Existing illustrations are unchanged. `home.arrow` visibly points up-right; its direction metadata declares that source orientation.

`assets/preview.png` is a deterministic light/dark gallery composition of the existing foreground artwork, final palette and native control glyphs. It is a concept preview, not a device screenshot.

## Composer parameters (1.1.6)

`assets/icon-parameters.png` is an existing original Muqun template glyph reused
from runtime-foundry. Creator: Muqun. Source:
https://github.com/osuki-dev/muqun-themes/tree/main/src/runtime-foundry/assets/icon-parameters.png
Icon license: CC-BY-4.0. The rest of this theme retains its existing licensing.

The two hexagonal knobs retain bold straight rails and give a different silhouette from settings’ rectangular stops. This transparent sliders mask is
independently declared as `chrome.parameters`, with template tinting in both
light and dark modes. It does not replace or fall back to `chrome.settings`.
No illustration, palette, surface opacity or composer decoration is changed.
