# Tiny Workshop

A miniature repair studio in pale concrete, painted tool blue and warm copper. Original robot mechanics work beside small drawers, lamps and believable hand tools. Dedicated portrait and landscape scenes place detailed workbenches near an edge, leaving broad quiet areas for the interface. The robots are an original collection of workshop characters rather than one fixed mascot.

Light surfaces resemble pale concrete with dark blue lettering and copper actions. Dark surfaces use deep tool blue, warm ivory text and luminous copper actions. Terminal colors retain distinct red, green, amber, blue, violet and cyan roles. Solid materials and restrained translucency preserve readability; the default Muqun name and logo remain unchanged.

The template glyphs use a wrench-arrow for back, a spring-driven dart for send and a nut-ended paperclip for attachments. The gallery cover combines daylight on the left and lamp-lit night on the right.
