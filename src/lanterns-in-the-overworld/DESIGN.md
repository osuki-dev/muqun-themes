# Lanterns in the Overworld

A miniature voxel forest connects mossy blocks, terracotta banks, timber shelters and amber lantern light. Four independently composed landscape and portrait scenes provide day and night settings. The cubic geometry remains visible instead of becoming a flat pixel pattern.

Light mode pairs warm limestone paper with deep moss actions; night uses smoky woodland surfaces and lantern-amber actions. UI opacity is 96% and terminal opacity 98%. Shared wallpaper is deliberately subdued, while Home uses stronger scenery plus a contained Steve-and-wolf hero. The default Muqun name and logo remain intact.

A stepped timber arrow, block-built send arrow and squared mine-rail paperclip retain familiar control shapes. The transparent lantern vignette belongs to empty states. The split gallery cover shows daylight on the left and night on the right.
