## Final cel artwork revision

The first new scene drafts had too much photographic texture and were rejected. The final assets below are built-in image_gen style-transfer redraws using those original AI-generated drafts as their only visual inputs, retaining scene composition while replacing textures with drawn outlines and flat cel values.

### Home

```text
Use case: style-transfer. Redraw this entire image in a visibly different, bold contemporary 2D ANIME CEL ANIMATION art style. Preserve the scene composition and main subjects: coral and cream train crossing cedar forest railway viaduct at mint dawn. Keep the original wide landscape canvas and genuine transparent alpha surroundings. This is NOT a photo filter. Completely replace every photographic/microtexture detail with hand-drawn design: decisive dark blue-teal INK OUTLINES around the major shapes; only TWO OR THREE FLAT COLOR VALUES per surface; enormous clean graphic color planes; crisp silhouette; bold saturated but tasteful coral, ice-blue or mint; sophisticated anime-film lighting shown as flat warm highlight planes. Water must be just 4–6 sweeping flat wave shapes and a few smooth white curves, NO realistic foam. Rocks become simple faceted polygon shapes, NO granules. Mountains become simple jagged outlined blue planes with broad white snow shapes. Cedars become large layered teal triangular graphic silhouettes, NO thousands of needles. Train and cabins use clear geometric line art and flat colored panels. All small photographic details are discarded; retain only meaningful large structure. Think premium hand-painted animation key art, genuinely drawn, youthful and graphic. NO photorealism, NO PBR materials, NO rendered3D, NO stock-photo texture, NO woodgrain, NO realistic reflections, NO gritty antique watercolor, NO tiny museum diorama. No text, numbers, logos, signs, borders or watermark. Generous foreground scale and full main subjects remain contained. The resulting image must immediately read as a drawn animation cel rather than a photograph. Transparent RGBA background.
```
