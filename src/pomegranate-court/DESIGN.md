# Pomegranate Court

Spain / Granada. Mineral terracotta and ivory in daylight, aubergine after dusk, with turquoise water and ceramic accents. Original imagined arcades and pomegranate gardens evoke local courtyard craft without copying a specific artwork.

Dedicated portrait and landscape day/night paintings keep the quiet upper center available for content. The gallery cover is a separate composition. A ceramic bowl and split pomegranate form the transparent empty-state vignette.

The template glyphs are a scalloped ceramic back chevron, an upward-right courtyard swallow for send, and a horseshoe-arch paperclip for attachment. Each is tintable in both modes. Default Muqun identity is retained.
