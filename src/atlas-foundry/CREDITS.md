# Atlas Foundry artwork

All six illustrations are original OpenAI image-generation works created for Muqun on 2026-09-26. Prompts specified a mature Black woman engineer in retro American industrial comic illustration, independent full-length launch and close Home compositions, and separate daylight and nighttime foundries. No existing characters, brands or lettering were requested. Source: original generated work, no external URL. Distributed under CC0-1.0.

The seven icons are original Muqun hex-bolt and forged-line geometry rasterized to PNG; CC0-1.0. The gallery preview combines this theme’s own artwork.

Foregrounds preserve genuine alpha and are capped at 1024 pixels on the longest edge, as are wallpapers. Full-length art includes both boots and all tools; no background rectangle is used.
